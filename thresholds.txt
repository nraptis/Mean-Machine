LOOP RECIPE THRESHOLDS
======================

Baseline
--------

These thresholds were derived from all 19,800 current heart/head/tail
recipes. The complete quartile and distance report is in:

    current_loop_flow_quartiles.txt

Percentile meaning
------------------

Quartiles remain diagnostics for comparing populations. They are no longer
independent acceptance demands. Requiring every metric to land on its best
20% side accepted 0 of 19,800 recipes because partially independent metrics
compound much faster than their individual percentiles suggest.

Refreshed percentile references
-------------------------------

The complete 19,800-recipe audit was rerun after the 33-expander export on
2026-08-02. The individual top-80% cutoffs (and the top-95% cutoff for
maximum orbiter shortest-path load) are recorded in both
current_loop_flow_quartiles.txt and knobs.json under
percentile_reference_thresholds. These are reference values for diagnostics
and ranking. They do not replace the looser simultaneous structural floors
below; applying every individual percentile cutoff as a hard gate still
over-constrains the joint population.

Policy
------

Hard validity rules reject malformed tails. Ascendency ranking now uses four
integer measurements only. The remaining measurements are diagnostics for
studying the graph; collecting a diagnostic does not give it a vote.

The four active measurements are:

    maximum single-orbiter ordered-pair damage (lower is better)
        Remove one non-root orbiter and count the destroyed ordered
        wanderer-to-wanderer relationships. This is the most direct test of
        concentration around one failure point.

    minimum surviving fanout after one orbiter removal (higher is better)
        After the same removal, find the weakest source and count how many
        other wanderers it still reaches. This prevents a good total-damage
        value from hiding one badly isolated source.

    minimum dual-tail wanderer reach (higher is better)
        Count destinations for which the weakest source reaches both tail
        operands before the operands reconverge. This directly measures
        braid-and-rejoin behavior in the actual ordered loop.

    maximum ordered source orphan area (lower is better)
        Execute the heart in order and sum how long the worst source remains
        separated from its destinations. This penalizes delayed diffusion,
        not merely eventual connectivity.

These measurements are integer counts derived from exact reachability. There
is no floating-point spectral estimate in active evaluation. The serialized
score record retains a zero-valued legacy slot only so existing binary farm
records keep their established layout.

For every heart, its 110 current recipes establish fixed cutoffs. Tier 0 means
that a candidate simultaneously reaches the best-five-percent side of all
four distributions; Tier 1 uses the best 20%; Tier 2 uses the better half.
The references never move while farming, so finding better candidates does
not move the goalposts.

The older global 25-metric threshold experiment accepted 482 of 19,800
recipes and left 132 heart buckets empty. Those figures explain why it was
replaced; they do not describe the Ascendency tier policy. The old threshold
table remains below as reproducible experimental history, not as the active
farmer's gate list.

Metric utility decisions
------------------------

Active evidence set
-------------------

The active four were retained because they describe separate, concrete
failure modes and all varied in each of five fixed-heart/fixed-head samples
of 50 random valid tails. Lexicographic ranking used single-orbiter damage
first; it made the first decision in all five samples. Surviving fanout,
dual-tail reach, and ordered orphan area each made a further reduction in two
of five samples. The average survivor counts after their successive decisions
were 4.0, 2.8, 2.4, and 1.0 candidates.

This is intentionally not a weighted composite. One excellent number cannot
pay for a bad number elsewhere. Tier gates require every measurement, then
the same four-value lexicographic order selects the best loop within a tier.

Static shortest-path load and redundant early-branch fraction remain useful
exploratory diagnostics, but no longer rank Ascendency candidates. Their
collapsed graph can traverse heart rounds out of execution order. The active
ordered metrics do not have that ambiguity.

Tier-yield check
----------------

The exact five-sample and farming results are recorded in
ascendency_metric_cut_analysis.txt. In brief, all four active metrics varied
in five out of five fixed-heart/fixed-head 50-tail samples. A full 550,000-tail
run on heart 6 placed all 110 tasks in Tier 0. The percentile intersection is
therefore selective but demonstrably farmable.

Weakest measurements
--------------------

The following should not be used to sell or rank the loops:

1. arrival total

   It is exactly 3,894 for all 19,800 recipes. It is an invariant/assertion,
   not a discriminator.

2. spectral gap

   It has only four values, 92.5% of the population has the same value, its
   effective cardinality is 1.33, and its maximum absolute correlation with
   another metric is only 0.019. In this graph family it sounds more
   informative than it is.

3. maximum dual-tail arrival skew

   It has three values, 97.1% of recipes tie at the mode, its effective
   cardinality is 1.15, and the median heart contains only one value. Keep it
   as a regression assertion, not as evidence of stronger flow.

The audit now emits a METRIC UTILITY DIAGNOSTICS table containing distinct
count, modal tie share, effective Shannon cardinality, median within-heart
cardinality, variance explained by heart identity, and maximum absolute
Spearman correlation. These statistical diagnostics do not establish
cryptographic strength; they establish whether a proposed structural metric
actually distinguishes the loop population and contributes independent
evidence.

Additional diagnostics worth retaining in reports:

    maximum orbiter shortest-path load
    minimum source orbiter journey area
    mean dual-tail reach
    mean and worst-source shortest path
    minimum subset expansion
    flow area
    minimum complement and dominated-pair count
    maximum source meeting area and mean pair-meeting latency
    maximum and mean robust-orphan count

Validity and regression floors, not ranking weights:

    minimum pair union
    total heart separation
    minimum four-edge reach
    redundant early-branch fraction
    minimum one-round fanout
    orphan straggler gap
    midpoint source, destination, and co-orphan loads
    zero source-pair meeting orphans

Diagnostic or disabled for this population:

    mean single-orbiter damage (rho=0.771 with mean robust orphans)
    maximum dual-tail arrival skew (11 in nearly the whole population)
    all constant influence/arrival/component metrics
    mean one-round fanout (rho=0.999 with its minimum)
    mean four-edge reach (rho=-0.787 with mean shortest path)
    directed diameter (coarse version of the path distribution)
    spectral gap (only four observed values)
    total subset expansion (useful report context, but its minimum is easier
    to interpret as a bottleneck floor)
    mean ordered source orphan area (the worst-source area is more protective)
    maximum last-orphan latency (rho=-0.965 with one-round fanout)

Structural difference thresholds
---------------------------------

heart minimum distance: 5
head minimum distance:  5
tail minimum distance: 13

Heart distance counts unequal canonical lead/source/feedback slots out of 33.
The current heart nearest-neighbor distribution is min/p25/p50/p75/max =
2/3/5/8/15, so 5 starts at its median rather than preserving the closest
current hearts.

Head distance counts unequal orbiter-to-wanderer assignments out of 11. Every
current head has nearest-neighbor distance 5, making 5 the direct preservation
threshold.

Tail distance counts unequal wanderer-update assignments out of 22. The
current nearest-neighbor range is 13..14, so 13 preserves the current floor.

Wanderer path graph
-------------------

The transition graph has 22 nodes: eleven wanderers and eleven orbiters. Its
directed edges spell out the real data-flow route:

    source wanderer -> head-assigned orbiter
    heart source orbiter -> lead orbiter -> feedback orbiter
    selected tail orbiters -> destination wanderer

Breadth-first search runs from every wanderer. Directed diameter remains the
longest individual shortest path. Two additional lower-is-better measurements
now cover the rest of the distance matrix:

    mean wanderer shortest path x1000
        Mean BFS distance over all 110 ordered pairs of distinct wanderers.
        Current min/p25/p50/p75/max = 3227/3318/3336/3363/3500.

    worst wanderer mean shortest path x1000
        For each source wanderer, mean distance to the other ten is measured;
        this is the worst of those eleven means. It prevents one peripheral
        wanderer from hiding behind a good global average.
        Current min/p25/p50/p75/max = 3300/3500/3500/3600/3900.

Both values are stored as fixed-point integers scaled by 1,000. The structural
floors are <=3363 and <=3700 respectively.

Every current source wanderer eventually influences all ten other wanderers.
That final count is therefore retained as a hard floor, but it cannot rank the
current recipes. Early reach is discriminating:

    minimum wanderers reached within four edges
        The weakest source reaches 6..10 other wanderers. Its current
        min/p25/p50/p75/max is 6/8/8/9/10; the floor is >=8.

    mean four-edge wanderer reach x1000
        Average early reach over all eleven sources. Its current
        min/p25/p50/p75/max is 8272/9181/9363/9545/10000. This metric is
        diagnostic because it has rho=-0.787 with mean shortest path.

The knottiness experiment counts, for every source/destination pair, whether
at least two different first orbiter branches can reach the destination within
the same bounded early window. Unlimited reach was rejected as a metric
because strong connectivity made it constant. The bounded version varies:

    redundant early-branch fraction x1000
        Current min/p25/p50/p75/max = 400/509/527/554/645.
        The structural floor is >=509.

This is a branch/reconvergence measurement, not a claim that the routes are
fully vertex-disjoint. Minimum subset expansion remains a separate global
measurement. Spectral gap is diagnostic only: it has just four distinct
values in this population and should not receive an acceptance threshold.

A literal average branch factor of a BFS tree is intentionally omitted. Every
spanning tree has the same edge count, and which equal-length parent BFS picks
depends on traversal order. Early reach measures the useful fan-out result;
the redundant early-branch fraction measures whether that spread has more than
one first branch instead of being carried by a single bottleneck route.

Ordered one-round journey
-------------------------

The ordered model starts each source wanderer in its head-assigned orbiter,
executes the eleven heart rounds in their real order, and then follows both
tail orbiters into each destination wanderer. This prevents a static cycle
from pretending that an edge was available earlier in the same iteration.

    minimum one-round wanderer fanout
        Other destinations reached by the weakest source in one iteration.
        Distribution = 7/10/10/10/10; floor >=9.

    minimum source orbiter journey area
        For each source, count how many orbiter slots carry it after every
        ordered heart round, then sum that curve. The weakest source wins.
        Distribution = 22/26/29/30/37; floor >=26.

    minimum and mean dual-tail wanderer reach
        Count destinations where a source reaches both tail operands before
        they recombine. This rewards fan-out followed by reconvergence, while
        minimum complement prevents overlap from replacing fresh material.
        Minimum floor >=1; mean x1000 floor >=572.

    maximum orbiter shortest-path load x1000
        Shortest paths are divided fractionally when alternatives exist. The
        busiest orbiter's share over all 110 ordered wanderer pairs is the
        score; lower means less dependence on one hub. Distribution =
        200/214/218/224/257; floor <=224.

Mean one-round fanout is disabled because its Spearman correlation with the
minimum is 0.999. Minimum one-round fanout is retained only as a weak inverse-
flow safety check; it must not receive a second score weight beside minimum
pair union, with which it has rho=0.896.

Ordered orphan and encounter tests
----------------------------------

The eleven-by-eleven ordered relationship matrix records the first completed
heart round in which each source wanderer reaches each destination wanderer.
An unreachable destination receives the sentinel round 12. This supports:

    maximum ordered source orphan area
        Sum the ten destination arrival rounds for each source and retain the
        worst source. This is the area under that source's orphan-count curve:
        every additional round of isolation adds one point per orphan.
        Distribution = 61/68/71/75/90; floor <=75.

    maximum orphan straggler gap
        For each source, subtract its second-latest arrival from its latest.
        This catches one relationship lagging behind an otherwise good list.
        Distribution = 0/2/3/3/7; floor <=3.

    midpoint orphan loads
        After five heart rounds, separately count the worst source's remaining
        orphans, the most-neglected destination, and the destination pair most
        often orphaned together. The floors are <=8, <=7, and <=6. The pair
        measurement is an early warning for a semi-isolated subflow.

The source-meeting matrix samples after each individual lead and feedback
operation. It records when every pair of original source wanderers first
coexists inside an orbiter:

    maximum source meeting orphans
        Must be zero. It is constant for the current recipes, but it is a
        useful validity test for newly generated recipes.

    maximum source meeting area
        Worst source's sum of first-meeting operation numbers.
        Distribution = 110/126/131/138/150; floor <=138.

    mean source-pair meeting latency x1000
        Global mean over all 110 directed source/partner relationships.
        Distribution = 8909/10181/10709/11127/11963; floor <=11127.

Robust-orphan testing removes every non-root orbiter role one at a time and
replays the ordered heart. A source/destination relationship is robust only if
at least one route survives every removal. The obligatory head-assigned source
orbiter is not removed. This is a structural bottleneck experiment, not a
literal cryptographic fault model:

    maximum robust orphans
        Worst source distribution = 6/7/8/8/9; floor <=8.

    mean robust orphans x1000
        Mean per-source distribution = 3636/5090/5363/5636/6909;
        floor <=5636.

Single-orbiter cut concentration
--------------------------------

The previous robust-orphan measurements ask whether each individual
source/destination relationship has any single-orbiter weak point. They do
not reveal whether the same orbiter is the shared weak point for many
relationships. The new cut experiment removes each non-root orbiter, replays
the ordered heart, and measures the whole source/destination matrix. A
source's mandatory head-assigned root orbiter is excluded because removing
that root trivially erases that source before the heart begins.

    maximum single-orbiter ordered-pair damage
        The largest number of ordered source/destination relationships lost
        to one removal. Distribution = 10/14/15/17/25. Lower is better.
        Unlike the mean measurements, it has no absolute Spearman
        correlation >=0.75 with an existing metric. It is therefore retained
        as a ranking discriminator, coarsened into two-relationship buckets
        so a one-pair difference cannot dominate the other flow tests.

    mean single-orbiter ordered-pair damage x1000
        Mean damage over all eleven possible removals. Distribution =
        5727/8909/9636/10363/14181. It remains diagnostic because it has
        rho=0.771 with mean robust-orphan count and would mostly score the
        same weakness twice.

    minimum surviving fanout after one orbiter removal
        Worst number of other destinations still reached by any source after
        any eligible removal. Distribution = 3/3/3/4/4. It is a useful
        coarse connectedness floor and a ranking refinement, but its two-value
        range is too small to carry a large independent weight.

    maximum dual-tail arrival skew
        Largest round gap between a source reaching the two tail operands of
        one destination. Distribution = 9/11/11/11/11. This experiment is
        retained for diagnostics but excluded from ranking because it is
        almost saturated and did not distinguish selected candidates.

In the 110-pair, 88,000-candidate random experiment, the connectedness-aware
Safe selection preserved minimum four-edge reach in all 110 pairs. Relative
to Exploration-best it improved maximum single-orbiter damage in 57 pairs,
tied 46, and regressed 7; minimum surviving fanout improved in 35, tied 70,
and regressed 5. All 11,000 Safe top-100 candidates remained inside their
corresponding Exploration top-500 pools.

The expanded structural policy currently retains 482 of 19,800 recipes
(2.43%). These tests are intentionally being kept separately for now. Later
correlation and farming-yield measurements can remove redundant tests rather
than collapsing them prematurely.

Hard failures
-------------

The following are validity requirements, not ranked scores:

1. The heart satisfies the canonical heart laws.
2. Every tail pair is allowed by the heart/head pair graph.
3. The complete transition graph is strongly connected.
4. The candidate clears the configured heart, head, and tail distance floors
   against the already accepted population.
5. The candidate clears every enabled metric threshold.

Feasibility
-----------

The explicit policy has demonstrated survivors. The random farmer must still
count and print rejections by individual threshold. Do not silently relax a
floor; use those rejection counts to make an explicit knobs.json change.
